Dear Editor,

Please consider our original research article, “An ESP32 Implementation Study of Pre-Provisioned Hybrid X25519 and ML-KEM-768 Key Exchange,” for publication in *SN Computer Science*.

The manuscript reports an open-source ESP32 testbed for an application-layer hybrid key-exchange deployment using pre-provisioned PSK authentication, X25519, and ML-KEM-768, followed by AES-GCM-protected telemetry and key renewal. Its contribution is an implementation and evidence package that makes the exchange, lifecycle, and measurement boundaries inspectable and reproducible. The manuscript does not propose a new cryptographic primitive, claim that this application-layer exchange is TLS, or claim a controlled performance advantage over TLS. Historical measurements and their limitations are identified separately.

We believe the work fits the journal’s coverage of computer and network security, cryptography, communication networks, and IoT systems. The manuscript is original, is not under consideration by another journal, and has been approved by all listed authors. The authors report no external funding. The corresponding author’s co-founder affiliation with Promaker Institute is disclosed in the manuscript; the authors report no other competing interests. The work did not involve human participants or animals. The authors reviewed the manuscript, figures, and supplementary materials and take responsibility for their accuracy.

The submission includes the editable manuscript sources, a vector Figure 1 file, and two cited supplementary resources. We intend to select the subscription publication route if the paper is accepted.
